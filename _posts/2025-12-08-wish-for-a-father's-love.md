title: "wish for a father's love"
author: "spencer"

---

there was so little touch
he sought a paternal embrace
in film

there was so little interest
he long forgot 
how small he made himself

until that night in the kitchen
silence stretching out between them
like a canyon

rage bubbling up
silenced by terror
him, passing by like I didn't exist

not even a glance
let alone a question
like his son was nothing

so his son felt like nothing
just a piece of trash
with a weakly beating heart

and just like that
it became disturbingly clear
so clear he crammed his eyes shut

this was the man
for all these years
who passed him by like nothing

this is the man
who's every day is 
just another normal day

just another normal day 
of not seeing his son
just trudging along

who could i ever be
without the love of a father
all these years and still

who could he be 
when he feels so small
that he's almost nothing at all

and yet somehow
no two days are the same
and none of them are lame

his son lives with a heart exposed
slashed, damaged, but bold
underfed, and diminished

but bold
bold
bold.
